Glass

Glass that reads on any backdrop

Frosted glass with a specular ambient glow works in every browser. Refraction bends what is behind the rim in Chromium browsers.

Try the lens

A glass lens follows your pointer over the picture. Turn the knobs to change its blur, glow and radius, and use the switch to turn refraction on or off. The markup under the tray updates to match.

Dark, light and blue glass

Dark is the default. Add fam-glass--light for light glass with dark text, or fam-glass--blue for blue glass with white text, which suits a primary or active control.

HTML
<div class="fam-glass"><input class="fam-glass-input" placeholder="Dark" aria-label="Dark"></div>
<div class="fam-glass fam-glass--light"><input class="fam-glass-input" placeholder="Light" aria-label="Light"></div>
<div class="fam-glass fam-glass--blue"><input class="fam-glass-input" placeholder="Blue" aria-label="Blue"></div>

Specular ambient glow every browser

The glass picks up the colors next to its rim and bleeds them inward. --fam-glow-depth sets how far.

HTML
<div class="fam-glass" style="--fam-glow-depth: 40px"></div>

Refraction

Chromium only Clear glass bends the grid behind its rim, and the bending follows the glass's own radius. That rounding comes from the optional fam-ui/refraction script. Other browsers show the same pane without the bending.

HTML
<!-- Paste snippets/refraction-filter.html once in the page, then: -->
<div class="fam-glass fam-glass--refract"></div>

<!-- Optional: bend along each glass's own radius -->
<script src="snippets/refraction-round.js" defer></script>
