Glass that reads on any backdrop

Frosted glass with a specular ambient glow works in every browser. Refraction bends what is behind the rim in Chromium browsers.

Frosted glass

Specular ambient glow every browser

The glass picks up the colors next to its rim and bleeds them inward. --fam-glow-depth sets how far.

Refraction Chromium only

Clear glass bends the grid behind its rim. Other browsers show the same pane without the bending.
