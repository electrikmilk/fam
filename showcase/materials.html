Materials

One class for every texture

Each material is a single class on any box. Felt, linen, denim and cardstock take a tint. Below the textures are the effects (gloss, rounding, shadows and reflections), the navigation bars, photo frames and engraved text made from them, each with its markup and a button that copies it.

Reflections

Reflections on a shelf

combine effects fam-pill fam-gloss-soft fam-round-md fam-shadow-md

reflections fam-reflect fam-shelf Put fam-reflect on a control, a photo or a frame for a glossy floor reflection that fades out below it, and fam-shelf on the box they stand in for a shiny floor. The reflection takes no layout space, so leave padding under it. Firefox does not support it and shows none.

cast and grounded shadows fam-cast-md fam-grounded-md Put either on an inline-block or block wrapper with the same radius when the element has shading of its own or clips its overflow, such as a button or the pill.
