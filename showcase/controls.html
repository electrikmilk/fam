Plastic and metal buttons

Raised plastic buttons in five colors. Round and square ones have a dish. A disabled button sinks into its case with a darker face and a muted label, like a button that has not popped up yet. They sit best on metal. Metal buttons are made the same way from brushed metal, and are shown on a metal plate.

A leather group (fam-button-group--leather) is the leather of the navigation bar, and its lit item is the same dark pocket.

Tabs

A tab strip is a button group with role="tablist", and each tab is a button with role="tab" and aria-selected. Silver glossy tabs follow the Snow Leopard strip, and plastic, metal, wood and leather tabs suit their materials. The wood and leather strips are the navigation bar's wood and leather. Arrow keys need a few lines of script, and tabs.js in the showcase folder is the one used here.

Switch and knob

The switch is a checkbox, so it toggles with no JavaScript. The knob is a picture, and --fam-knob-angle turns its tick.
