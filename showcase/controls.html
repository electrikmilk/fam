Plastic and metal buttons

Raised plastic buttons in five colors. Round and square ones have a dish. A disabled button sinks into its case with a darker face and a muted label, like a button that has not popped up yet. They sit best on metal. Metal buttons are made the same way from brushed metal, and are shown on a metal plate.

Glossy and glass buttons

Glossy buttons are the aqua pill on a button, in blue and silver, and fam-gloss-soft or fam-gloss-strong dial the sheen. Glass buttons are the frosted pill on a button and read best on a dark surface.

Switch and knob

The switch is a checkbox, so it toggles with no JavaScript. The knob is a picture, and --fam-knob-angle turns its tick.

HTML
<div class="fam-metal" style="display: flex; flex-wrap: wrap; gap: 28px; align-items: center; padding: 28px">
  <label class="fam-switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" aria-label="On example" checked><span class="fam-switch-knob"></span></label>
  <label class="fam-switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" aria-label="Off example"><span class="fam-switch-knob"></span></label>
  <label class="fam-switch"><input type="checkbox" role="switch" aria-label="Disabled example" disabled><span class="fam-switch-knob"></span></label>
  <span class="fam-knob" role="img" aria-label="Volume low" style="--fam-knob-angle: -100deg"></span>
  <span class="fam-knob" role="img" aria-label="Volume medium"></span>
  <span class="fam-knob" role="img" aria-label="Volume high" style="--fam-knob-angle: 100deg"></span>
</div>
