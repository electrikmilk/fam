Buttons

Every button is a real button element with a dish, a case and a disabled look that sinks into its surface. Pick the family that matches the surface it sits on, then add a shape or a color.

ClassWhat it does
fam-plasticRaised plastic button. Add a color: fam-plastic--red, fam-plastic--green, fam-plastic--amber, fam-plastic--blue, fam-plastic--purple or fam-plastic--cream.
fam-plastic--roundAn icon button with a round dish. fam-plastic--square is the same with a square case.
fam-metal-buttonButton in the metal of fam-metal. fam-metal-button--round and fam-metal-button--square are its icon shapes.
fam-metal-button--brushedSwaps the metal for the coarser brushed metal of fam-metal-brushed.
fam-wood-buttonButton in light wood with dark ink. fam-wood-button--round and fam-wood-button--square are its icon shapes.
fam-wood-button--paneledSwaps the light wood for the dark planks of fam-wood-paneled, with light ink.
fam-leather-buttonTan leather button. It fills the width of its container, so put it in a box of the width you want.
fam-glossy-buttonThe aqua pill. fam-glossy-button--silver is the silver one, and fam-gloss-soft and fam-gloss-strong dial its sheen.
fam-glass-buttonThe frosted pill. Use it with fam-glass, and add fam-glass--light or fam-glass--blue to tint it. It reads best on a dark surface.
fam-button-groupPuts buttons in one case. Mark the lit one with aria-pressed="true". fam-button-group--case is a raised case, fam-button-group--leather a leather one and fam-button-group--wood a wooden one.

Button groups

Buttons can share one case. Wrap them in a group, and mark the lit one with aria-pressed="true". A lit square plastic button shows its LED in the dish, and the others show the dark lens. Labeled buttons show a small lens above the label, dark until they are lit. The LED fades in and out. A leather group (fam-button-group--leather) is the leather of the navigation bar, and its lit item is the same dark pocket.

Radio buttons

Real radio inputs, so arrow keys and forms work with no JavaScript. Plastic and metal radios are LEDs that are dark when unchecked and glow when checked, and a disabled one sinks into its case. Glossy and glass radios show a plain dot.

ClassWhat it does
fam-radioA label that wraps a real radio input and a fam-radio-dot. Arrow keys and forms work with no JavaScript.
fam-radio--plasticAn LED that is dark when unchecked and glows when checked. fam-radio--metal is the same on metal.
fam-radio--glossyA plain dot for the glossy pill. fam-radio--glass is the plain dot for glass, and the checked one turns blue.

Tabs

A tab strip is a button group with role="tablist", and each tab is a button with role="tab" and aria-selected. Silver glossy tabs follow the Snow Leopard strip, and plastic, metal, wood and leather tabs suit their materials. The wood and leather strips are the navigation bar's wood and leather. Arrow keys need a few lines of script, and tabs.js in the showcase folder is the one used here.

ClassWhat it does
fam-button-groupThe tab strip. Give it role="tablist" and each button role="tab" with aria-selected.
fam-glossy-button--silverSilver glossy tabs follow the Snow Leopard strip.
fam-button-group--caseA padded plastic case. Fill it with fam-plastic--cream buttons.
fam-button-group--woodThe wooden navigation bar's wood. Put fam-wood on the group and fam-nav-item on each tab.
fam-button-group--leatherThe leather navigation bar's leather, with fam-leather-button tabs.

fam-dialThe plastic dial, drawn in CSS. It sits on fam-plastic-panel.

Switches and toggles

A switch is a checkbox, so it toggles with no JavaScript. A toggle LED is a label that wraps a checkbox: it is lit while checked, works in forms, and takes its name from its label.

ClassWhat it does
fam-switchA label that wraps a checkbox with role="switch" and a fam-switch-knob.
fam-ledA label that wraps a checkbox with role="switch" is a toggle LED. Add a color with fam-led--green, fam-led--red or fam-led--blue and a size with fam-led--large.

LEDs

Standalone indicator lamps for the plastic panel and the metal. Add fam-led--lit to light one, and fam-led--green, --red or --blue to change its color (amber is the default). fam-led--large makes it bigger. The label is engraved unless it has fam-led-label--printed, and the lens is a photo of a real lamp tinted by CSS. The lens is decorative, so say the state in fam-visually-hidden text.

ClassWhat it does
fam-ledA standalone indicator lamp with a fam-led-lens and a fam-led-label. Amber by default.
fam-led--litLights the lamp. fam-led--green, fam-led--red and fam-led--blue change its color, and fam-led--large makes it bigger.

Zebra tables

A table in a box of fixed height. The header stays at the top while the rows scroll, and the stripes carry on below the last row so the box always looks full. The stripes are a see-through tint of one ink, so the paper or cardstock shows through them. --fam-row-height sets the height of every row and of the empty stripes, --fam-table-rows sets how many rows tall the box is, and --fam-table-ink and --fam-table-stripe set the color and strength of the stripes. Rows are one line, so text that does not fit is cut short with an ellipsis. Light ink is for dark materials such as brown cardstock. Small dark text does not reach 4.5:1 on the natural light cardstock, whose fibers are dark flecks, so put dark ink on paper and use light ink on brown cardstock. The first table is read only. The second is a grid with a selected row, and its arrow keys need a few lines of script, which table-grid.js in the showcase folder supplies.
