What changed

  • Opening screen is tan stitched leather with the title engraved into it, instead of the photo.
  • The opening screen text is serif (Georgia, then Times New Roman), the same face as the sign-in card title.
  • New photo frames section after the metal controls: print frames with a paper margin and a cardstock margin, a wood frame and a glossy wood frame, and a corkboard in a wood frame.
  • Build your own gains cork and tinted cardstock (natural by default, brown with --fam-cardstock-tint).
  • Engraved text: one panel each for metal, leather and wood.
  • New "build your own" section before the glass: a square for every texture, felt in five tints, then gloss, rounding and shadow utilities (shadows are my guess).
  • The ribbon photo now belongs to the glass section only, which runs edge to edge on a dark background.

My guess: knobs, not sliders

  • The kit has no slider, so the glass controls are the metal knobs you would turn (blur, glow, corner radius) plus a switch for frosted or refracting.
  • The knob's reflections stay fixed while the orange tick turns, like a real knob under a light.
  • Turning a knob updates the CSS variables shown with the lens, so people can copy them.
